Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the "clipper" app used by the channels Fichado na Live and Mugshot Daily (together, "we"). The app is a private publishing tool: its only users are the owner of these two channels.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts (account name and ID), used only to confirm that each video goes to the right account.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to those same accounts, and to read the status of the videos we upload.

The app does not access, collect or store data from any other person, follower or viewer.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are never sold, shared or sent to third parties, except to the platform that issued them (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram, TikTok) when making API requests.

Deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time in the security settings of the Google, Instagram or TikTok account. To ask for deletion of any data, write to the contact below; we answer within 30 days.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos to our own YouTube channels and to read the channel name and the processing status of the videos we upload. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data received from YouTube is handled as described in this policy and in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app keeps only the OAuth access and refresh tokens on the owner's computer. It does not keep any other data from YouTube; video status is read only to confirm an upload, and nothing from YouTube is stored for more than 30 days. You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings > Third-party access; the stored tokens are then useless and are deleted within 7 days.
